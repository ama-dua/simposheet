### Description
SimpoSheet is a web application I founded and built to convert text-based PDF bank statements, receipts, and bills into clean, ready-to-use Excel workbooks.

The converter creates structured worksheets with real Excel dates and currency values. Bank statement conversions include debit/credit separation and a reconciliation check. Receipt conversions produce a single clean expense record. Bill conversions extract the summary, previous balance, current charges, total due, and supported line items, then verify those figures against the bill's own totals.

I designed the product around trust and usability: uploaded files are processed temporarily and deleted after conversion, no document database is used, and all portfolio examples use fictional data.

Technologies: Python, Flask, Gunicorn, pdfplumber, OpenPyXL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
