Questions

Before you upload

Do you store my documents?

No. The uploaded PDF and generated workbook are held only in a temporary working folder for the conversion and deleted when the download response closes.

What kinds of PDFs work best?

Text-based PDFs downloaded from a bank, business, or service provider. Scanned images require OCR, which is a future feature.

What is inside the Excel file?

Formatted worksheets using real Excel date and currency values, so you can sort, filter, total, and build formulas immediately.
