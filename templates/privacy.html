SimpoSheet Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

SimpoSheet converts PDF bank statements, receipts, and bills into clean, formatted Excel workbooks. Protecting the privacy of the documents you convert is the core of how the service is built — not an afterthought.

Your documents

  • When you upload a PDF, it is processed on our server only to create your Excel workbook.
  • Your uploaded file and the generated workbook are held only in temporary storage during conversion and are deleted immediately after your download is delivered.
  • We do not keep copies of your documents, and we do not read, mine, or share their contents for any other purpose.

Information we collect

  • SimpoSheet currently has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, or payment information to convert a document.
  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as timestamps and error records) needed to operate and secure the service. Document contents and conversion details are not written to those logs.
  • SimpoSheet does not use advertising trackers or sell personal information.

Cookies and local storage

The SimpoSheet web app may store its interface files (such as styles and icons) in your browser so the installed app opens quickly. Your documents and conversions are never stored in your browser by the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, when optional user accounts or subscriptions are introduced — the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
